# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Mobdesk a welcoming, respectful
and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background,
identity, experience level or technical skill.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus on the technical problem, its impact and possible solutions.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Protect private information and do not publish secrets.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption, trolling or repeated bad-faith participation.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to
[contato@ericklucioh.com](mailto:contato@ericklucioh.com). Reports will be
reviewed as confidentially as practical. The maintainer may remove comments,
close discussions, restrict participation or take other appropriate action.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is not
acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies to project spaces, issue and pull request discussions,
community interactions representing Mobdesk and any event where a person is
representing the project.

## Attribution

This policy is based on the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
